## 🎯 1. Background

In the era of rising living costs and inflation, personal financial management is no longer optional – it’s essential.  
This project aims to **analyze financial behaviors** at both the community and personal levels to answer:

> *“Are we earning, spending, and saving in line with our goals – and how do we compare with our industry & demographic group?”*

---

## ❓ 2. Business Questions

1. What are the average income, expenses, and savings across the community?  
2. How do occupation and age affect saving capacity?  
3. How is spending split between essential and non-essential categories?  
4. Where does one individual stand compared to their peers?  
5. How far is the actual financial behavior from the recommended target?  
6. How can businesses use this data to provide better financial products?

| Savings %               | ≥ 15%   | -22%    | -37%     |

💡 **Insight:** Large gap from the recommended benchmarks → needs urgent adjustment.  

---

## 🏁 6. Conclusion & Business Use Cases  

**For Individuals:**  
- Reduce non-essential spending by at least 15%.  
- Automate 10–15% income savings.  
- Reassess large fixed costs.

**For Businesses:**  
- **Banks/Fintech:** Offer auto-saving products, unusual spending alerts.  
- **Insurance:** Target high-income but low-saving individuals.  
- **Financial Advisors:** Use industry benchmarks to create personalized financial plans.
